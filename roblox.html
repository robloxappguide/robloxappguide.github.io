How to Play Roblox: A Beginner's Guide

From installing the app to joining your first experience in about five minutes — here is everything a new player (or a parent setting up an account) needs to know.

Step 1: Install the official app

1

Download from the official store only

On Android, open Google Play; on iPhone or iPad, open the App Store. Search for Roblox and check that the publisher is Roblox Corporation, then install it free. The same account also works on PC, Mac, Xbox and PlayStation.

AndroidGoogle Play iPhone & iPadApp Store

Step 2: Create your account

2

Sign up with a real birth date

Open the app and tap Sign Up. Enter your date of birth, choose a username that does not contain your real name, and set a strong, unique password. The birth date matters: Roblox uses it to apply the right chat filters and content settings, so always enter the true one. Parents — add your email address in account settings so the account can be recovered later.

3

Turn on 2-Step Verification right away

Go to Settings → Security and enable 2-Step Verification. From then on, logging in on a new device needs a code sent to your email or authenticator app. It takes one minute and blocks almost every account-theft attempt.

Step 3: Build your avatar

4

Meet your avatar

Your avatar is the character you play as in every experience. Tap the Avatar tab to change body type, skin tone, hair, face and clothing. Every account starts with a free set of items, and the Marketplace holds millions of community-made items — many of them free. Outfits can be saved, so most players keep a few looks and swap between them.

Step 4: Find an experience and play

5

Pick a game from the Discover page

The Discover (or Home) tab shows trending, top-rated and recommended experiences. Tap any tile to see its description, player count and rating, then press the green Play button. You will drop into a live server with other players within seconds. Not sure where to start? Brookhaven RP for relaxed roleplay, Tower of Hell for a challenge, and Adopt Me! for pets are the classic first stops.

6

Learn the basic controls

On mobile, move with the on-screen joystick and tap the jump button; swipe to move the camera. On PC, use WASD to move, Space to jump and the mouse to look around. Most experiences add their own buttons and a short in-game tutorial — look for a "?" or menu icon in the corner of the screen.

Step 5: Add friends

7

Play better together

Search for a friend's username under Friends and send a request. Once accepted, you can see which experience they are in and join the same server with one tap. In-game, the chat button lets your party talk while you play (chat features vary by age setting).

For parents: five-minute setup checklist

1) Use your child's real birth date. 2) Add your own email for recovery. 3) Set an Account Controls PIN under Settings → Parental Controls so settings can't be changed without it. 4) Review who can chat with and invite your child under Privacy. 5) Set a monthly spending limit if you allow Robux purchases. Our Tips & Tricks page explains each setting in detail.

Roblox Tips & Tricks

Practical advice that experienced players wish someone had told them on day one — staying safe, understanding Robux, and finding the games actually worth your time.

Stay safe: settings that matter

Lock down your privacy settings

Under Settings → Privacy you control who can message you, invite you to private servers, join you in experiences and see your inventory. For younger players, setting these to Friends (or even No one for messages) removes most unwanted contact while keeping the game fully playable.

Use the Account Controls PIN

Settings → Parental Controls lets a parent set a 4-digit PIN. Once set, nobody can change privacy, spending or allowed-experience settings without it — including the child on a borrowed device.

Know the red flags

  • "Free Robux" generators, links and videos are always scams. They exist to steal accounts. Robux only ever comes from the official app, stores or a Premium membership.
  • Never type your password anywhere except the real Roblox app or roblox.com — and never share your browser cookie with anyone, for any reason.
  • If a stranger offers an amazing trade or asks you to "verify" on another site, it is a theft attempt. Block, report and move on.
  • Roblox staff will never message you in-game asking for your password or items.

Robux basics, explained honestly

Robux is Roblox's in-game currency. It buys avatar clothing and accessories, game passes (permanent perks inside one experience), private servers and developer products. A few ground rules:

  • You don't need Robux to have fun. Playing experiences is free, and thousands of avatar items cost nothing.
  • Buy it only in the app or official stores. Third-party "cheap Robux" sites are scams or sell stolen currency that gets accounts banned.
  • Premium membership gives a monthly Robux allowance plus trading and selling perks — worth it only if you play and trade a lot.
  • Creators earn Robux by selling clothing, items and game passes, and can convert earnings to real money through the Developer Exchange once they qualify.
  • Gift cards from normal retailers are a safe way for parents to give Robux without storing a card in the app.

Finding genuinely good games

Read the like ratio, not just the player count

A big player count means a game is popular today; the thumbs-up percentage tells you whether people actually enjoyed it. As a rule of thumb, experiences above 85% positive with tens of thousands of votes are reliably good.

Check the update date

Open the experience page and look at when it was last updated. Active developers update weekly or monthly; a game untouched for two years may still work, but events and fixes have stopped.

Use charts and genres properly

The Discover page's charts — Top Rated, Trending, Up-and-Coming — are far better hunting grounds than random search results. When you find a game you love, its page recommends similar ones, and following the same creator often leads to their other, smaller gems.

Try before you spend

Game passes are permanent but non-refundable, and they only work inside one experience. Play a game for a few sessions first; if you are still playing it next week, the pass is probably worth it.

Performance tips for phones

  • In any experience, open Settings (Esc or the menu icon) and set Graphics Mode to Manual, then lower the slider if the game stutters — it is the single biggest fix for lag.
  • Play on Wi-Fi where possible; Roblox streams the world as you move, so a weak mobile signal causes rubber-banding.
  • Close other apps before playing on older phones, and keep the Roblox app updated — performance fixes ship constantly.

The 10-second rule for any Roblox link

Before tapping any link that promises Robux, rare items or account help, ask: is this inside the official app or on roblox.com? If the answer is no, close it. That one habit prevents nearly every common Roblox scam.

About This Guide

A fan-made companion for one of the biggest gaming platforms on Earth.

Roblox launched in 2006 and has grown from a small physics playground into a platform where tens of millions of people play, build and hang out every day. Its library is not made by one studio — it is made by its own community, which is why there is always something new and why finding the good stuff can feel overwhelming at first.

This site exists to make that first step easier. We write plain-English guides for new players and parents: how to install the official app safely, how accounts, avatars and Robux actually work, which settings keep younger players protected, and which experiences are worth your first hours.
